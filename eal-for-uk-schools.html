Strengthening EAL provision across UK schools

The number of multilingual learners in the UK continues to grow, creating a complex challenge for schools. Learners may arrive at different points in the academic year, with varying levels of English proficiency and often require targeted support alongside the demands of the wider curriculum. Without a clear, consistent approach, EAL provision can become fragmented, relying heavily on individual staff expertise and limited capacity.

EAL provision and Ofsted

Alongside these challenges, expectations around inclusion and accountability have increased. Under Ofsted’s inspection framework, schools are expected to demonstrate how they identify EAL learners, understand their needs and support progress over time as part of an inclusive, whole-school approach.
